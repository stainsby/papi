## Success indicators

[Things you can see at project level — not story-level acceptance
criteria. How would we know, from a step back, that the project is on
its way to its Vision and is doing it in keeping with its Ethos?

Charter-level indicators are the kind of thing you'd notice over
months or years, often *about* the project rather than *inside* it —
adoption, reputation, contributor health, or the absence of certain
kinds of failure.

NOT charter-level: "feature X is shipped", "bug count under N", "test
coverage above Y%". Those belong on stories or sprints.

Some examples to draw from:

* "Other teams adopt our work without being asked."
* "New contributors get productive within a week."
* "We have not had to apologise to a user for a regression in six months."
* "Outside contributors open PRs without needing hand-holding."
* "Findings are reproduced by at least one independent group."
* "Users return without being prompted."
]
